ZERO TOLERANCE POLICY

KPay maintains a zero-tolerance approach toward all forms of fraud, bribery, and financial misconduct. This is not just a policy — it is a core condition of your employment.

🎯
Why This Training Matters

As a payment service provider operating across multiple markets, KPay sits at the intersection of merchant transactions, cardholder data, and financial infrastructure. This makes us — and you — high-value targets for fraud.

🔍
Recognize fraud signals early

Identify red flags before they become incidents — in your own workflow and those around you.

🛡️
Protect KPay and our merchants

Understand your role in maintaining the integrity of our payment infrastructure.

📣
Speak up with confidence

Know exactly how to report concerns — anonymously if needed — without fear of retaliation.

💡

Did You Know?

According to global occupational fraud studies, the median loss from internal fraud is US$145,000, and it takes an average of 12 months to detect. In the payments industry, the average data breach costs US$5.9 million. The earlier you spot it, the smaller the damage.

👥
Who This Policy Applies To

This policy covers all KPay personnel globally, without exception:

Full-time Employees Management & Trainees Contractors Temporary Staff Third-Party Vendors All Operating Regions
Note for managers: You carry additional responsibility. If a team member raises a concern, you are obligated to escalate — not dismiss.
📖
How Fraud Is Defined at KPay

Fraud is any intentional act or omission designed to deceive others, resulting in loss to the victim or gain to the perpetrator. This includes conduct that is:

🔴 Deliberate 🔴 Reckless 🔴 Negligent
Key principle: "I didn't mean to" or "I didn't know" are not defenses under this policy. Negligence that enables fraud is treated the same as active participation.

5 Categories of Internal Fraud at KPay
💸 Misappropriation of Assets ▼
Theft or unauthorized use of KPay's funds, equipment, or intellectual property. This includes: processing unauthorized refunds to personal accounts, manipulating settlement records to divert funds, using KPay payment infrastructure for personal gain, and misusing company-issued devices or software licences.
🔐 Data Breach & Information Theft ▼
Accessing data beyond your job scope, exfiltrating merchant data to personal devices or external platforms, or sharing system credentials with unauthorized persons. Employees must never access merchant, client, or commercial data outside their assigned responsibilities.
🎁 Bribery & Corruption ▼
Accepting or offering kickbacks, gifts, or favors to influence business decisions or bypass compliance checks. This applies regardless of value, and regardless of whether the benefit is received directly or through a connected person.
📄 Falsification of Records ▼
Forging or altering any company document with intent to deceive — including financial statements, KYC files, expense reports, transaction records, compliance records, and any document submitted to a regulator or auditor. This also includes impersonating a client or counterparty signature.
🤝 Conflict of Interest Non-Disclosure ▼
Failing to disclose a personal, financial, or professional interest in a merchant, vendor, or counterparty where it could influence business decisions. This includes undisclosed relationships with merchant applicants, referral arrangements conferring personal benefit, and ownership stakes in competing businesses. Employees must not share KPay's merchant lists or pipeline data with third parties.

External & Operational Fraud

Module 3: Fraud threats that exploit KPay from the outside
Module 3 / 5
🌐
What Is External Fraud?

External fraud is perpetrated by merchants, customers, cardholders, third-party agents, or organised criminal networks that exploit KPay's products or payment infrastructure — sometimes in collusion with KPay employees.

Critical: Collusion between internal staff and external parties is the most serious form of misconduct under this policy.
🎯

Social Engineering — You May Be a Target

Fraudsters increasingly use social engineering tactics targeting employees: CEO fraud emails impersonating executives to demand urgent payments, AI deepfakes mimicking a manager's voice or video to authorize transactions, and phishing emails requesting system credentials. Never bypass formal approval processes — even if an instruction appears to come from senior leadership.

📋
External Fraud Typologies

Collusion with merchants to process unauthorized transactions, facilitating transaction laundering, fabricating transactions, or ignoring red flags during onboarding.

Employees in merchant-facing roles (BD, Operations, CS) must complete enhanced training before independently managing merchant accounts.

Intentionally bypassing AML protocols, KYC checks, or payment gateway security controls — including approving non-compliant merchants through negligence, wilful blindness, or active collusion.

"Wilful blindness" — deliberately ignoring suspicious signs — carries the same legal weight as active participation in fraud.

Assisting merchants to apply using a false corporate identity, fabricated business, or by impersonating a legitimate brand or franchise without authorization.

Using encrypted messaging platforms, online forums, or dark web marketplaces to distribute KPay payment links, compromised cardholder data, or credentials used to facilitate fraud.

Any employee found sharing KPay payment links or credentials via personal messaging apps faces immediate investigation and potential termination.

Sanctions Screening is a mandatory obligation in KPay's merchant onboarding and ongoing transaction monitoring. No merchant application may be approved or transaction released without completing a sanctions check.

Sanctioned entities include individuals, businesses, or countries subject to UN, US Treasury OFAC, EU, or HKMA sanctions. Any business dealings with sanctioned entities are unlawful and must be reported to Compliance immediately.

Compliance & Security Standards

Module 4: The regulatory frameworks you must operate within
Module 4 / 5
🔒
Your Four Compliance Pillars
💳 PCI DSS — Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard ▼
Adherence to PCI DSS is non-negotiable. Any attempt to circumvent cardholder data environment (CDE) controls, bypass encryption protocols, or mishandle Primary Account Numbers (PAN) constitutes gross misconduct. All in-scope employees — not just IT — are bound by this.
🏦 AML / CTF — Anti-Money Laundering & Counter-Terrorist Financing ▼
You must comply with all applicable AML, CTF, sanctions, and financial crime laws in every jurisdiction KPay operates. This includes customer due diligence, reporting suspicious activities, and cooperating with regulatory investigations. Failure to file a required Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) is itself an offence.
🛡️ Data Protection — Privacy & Information Security ▼
Personal data must be accessed and processed on a need-to-know basis only. System access logs are actively monitored and anomalous access patterns trigger automatic investigation. There is no "private browsing" within KPay's systems.
🌏 Regulatory Alignment — Cross-Border Operations ▼
If you work on product localization or overseas expansion, every initiative must comply with Hong Kong regulations (including SVF requirements) and the local regulations of the target market. You cannot launch first and comply later.
👤
Your Personal Obligations
1

Data Minimization

Only access data strictly necessary for your immediate job function. Even if the system technically permits broader access, accessing beyond your role is a violation.

2

Separation of Duties

No single employee should control a complete transaction cycle without independent review. You must not approve your own work or override controls you are subject to.

3

Vigilance

Stay alert to anomalies: unusual chargeback ratios, irregular database queries, or any instruction to skip a step or avoid documentation. Any instruction designed to circumvent accountability is itself a red flag.

4

Mandatory Training

Annual fraud awareness and information security training is mandatory and tracked. Merchant-facing roles require additional modules before independently managing merchant accounts.

Reporting & Consequences

Module 5: How to speak up, and what happens when fraud is confirmed
Module 5 / 5
📣
"See Something, Say Something"

If you suspect fraudulent activity, you have a duty to report it immediately. Silence is not neutral — it enables fraud to continue and may implicate you.

👔
Line Manager / Dept Head

First point of contact for most concerns. Suitable for initial escalation.

🏛️
Internal Audit & Compliance

For concerns involving senior staff or where a conflict of interest exists.

🔒
Whistleblower Portal

Anonymous reporting via KPay's designated Lark portal. Fully confidential.

🛡️
Non-Retaliation Guarantee
You are fully protected. KPay strictly prohibits retaliation against any employee who raises a genuine concern in good faith. Any employee who retaliates against a whistleblower is subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination.

This protection applies to good-faith reports. It does not protect employees who knowingly make false or malicious allegations — this is also treated as misconduct.

⚡
Consequences of Confirmed Fraud — Click Each Item to Expand

Once fraud is confirmed, the consequences are immediate and multi-layered.

🚪 Immediate Termination
Effective Immediately
Proven fraudulent activity constitutes gross misconduct, resulting in immediate termination with no notice period. KPay reserves the right to suspend an employee with pay during investigation to protect system integrity and company assets.
📉 ESOP & Incentives Forfeited
Effective Immediately
Upon termination for gross misconduct or fraud, all unvested company incentives — including ESOP allocations — are immediately forfeited with no compensation.
⚖️ Criminal Prosecution
Where Applicable
KPay will report confirmed fraud to law enforcement and regulatory authorities for criminal prosecution or civil recovery — including against former employees, co-conspirators, and any third party who benefited.
💰 Clawback of Earnings
Where Applicable
Where any discretionary payment — including commissions, bonuses, or incentive awards — is found to have been earned through fraudulent conduct, KPay reserves the right to seek full recovery through civil proceedings or any other available legal remedy.
